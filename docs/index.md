# Fern

Fern is a desktop songwriting notebook for Windows. Keep lyrics, chords, notes, checklists and recordings together for each song. Fern needs a Fern account, and your library syncs to your other devices through it; once you are signed in, you can keep working offline. You can also link demo audio from Google Drive or SoundCloud: with Google Drive, you pick a file and Fern plays it inside the app.

**Download:** [Fern releases on GitHub](https://github.com/stevenuocaster-arch/fern-releases/releases)
